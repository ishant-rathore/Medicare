# Medicare — Community Interview Questionnaire

## Purpose

Use this questionnaire to understand the practical medication-reminder, accessibility, voice and navigation needs of senior citizens. Ask only what is necessary for the project. Do not collect diagnoses, prescription details or other unnecessary medical information.

## Questions

1. How many different medicines do you usually take in a day?
2. At what times do you normally take them?
3. How often do you accidentally forget or delay a dose?
4. Do you prefer written, sound or spoken reminders?
5. Which language would you prefer for a spoken reminder?
6. Can you easily read normal smartphone notification text?
7. Which controls, buttons or screen elements are difficult to use?
8. Would a family member's recorded voice make a reminder more useful?
9. What should happen if you do not respond to a reminder?
10. Would vibration or a full-screen visual reminder help you notice a dose?
11. Would seeing a medicine photo, color or shape help you identify it?
12. Is there anything about current medicine reminder apps that feels confusing or difficult?
