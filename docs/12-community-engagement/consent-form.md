# Medicare — CEP Participant Consent Form

## Project

**Medicare — Voice Reminders for Senior Citizen Medications**  
Community Engagement Project (CEP)

## Purpose

This consent form is for participation in interviews, usability activities and/or the short community trial of the Medicare medication reminder prototype.

## What participation involves

The participant may be asked to:

- discuss general medication-reminder habits and accessibility preferences;
- try selected Medicare screens and reminder interactions;
- provide usability feedback;
- participate in a short prototype deployment/trial where applicable.

Participation should focus on product usability and reminder experience. The project should avoid collecting unnecessary diagnoses, prescription details or other sensitive medical information.

## Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary. The participant may refuse to answer a question or stop participation at any time without penalty.

## Data and Privacy

The project team will:

- collect only information reasonably required for the study;
- prefer anonymous participant IDs in notes and reports;
- use aggregated or anonymized findings where practical;
- protect any approved recordings/photos and use them only for the agreed project purpose;
- not publish identifying information without appropriate authorization.

## Medical Safety

Medicare is a medication reminder and organization tool. It is not a diagnostic, prescribing or clinical decision-support system and does not replace professional healthcare advice.
